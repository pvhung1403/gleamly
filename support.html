Support

Need help? Open an issue at github.com/pvhung1403/gleamly/issues and we'll get back to you.

Does Gleamly upload my photos?

Never. Duplicate detection, similarity scoring, blur detection and contact checks run 100% on your iPhone.

Can I recover photos after cleaning?

Yes. Deleted photos and videos move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

Why doesn't storage go down right after cleaning?

iOS keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Empty that album in Photos to free the space immediately.

Why doesn't Gleamly clear other apps' caches?

iOS sandboxes every app. Gleamly follows Apple's rules and only works with the Photo Library and Contacts you authorize.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel anytime in iOS Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal. To restore, open the Pro screen in Gleamly and tap Restore Purchases.
