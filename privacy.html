Gleamly

Storage Cleaner for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Short version: Gleamly analyzes your photos, videos and contacts entirely on your iPhone. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Nothing ever leaves your device.

1. Data we access

  • Photo Library (only with your permission, full or limited): used to find duplicate, similar, blurry and large photos, screenshots and videos, and to estimate the storage they use.
  • Contacts (only with your permission): used to find duplicate and incomplete contact cards and to merge or delete them when you ask.
  • Device storage capacity: total and available space, read from iOS to show your storage overview.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings › Gleamly.

2. How the data is processed

All analysis runs locally using Apple frameworks (PhotoKit, Vision, AVFoundation and Contacts). Photos, videos, contacts, image fingerprints and analysis results are never uploaded to any server — Gleamly has no server.

3. Deletions and changes

Photos and videos are deleted only after you confirm in Apple's native system dialog. Deleted items move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where they can be recovered for 30 days. Contact merges and deletions happen only after you confirm inside the app. Optional HEVC video compression saves a new copy to your library and never removes the original without your confirmation.

4. Data stored on your device

Gleamly keeps a small cleanup history (action name, item count, space freed and date), your appearance preference and onboarding state in the app's local storage on your iPhone. This data is removed when you delete the app.

5. Purchases

Gleamly Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. No tracking, ads or analytics

Gleamly contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not create user accounts.

7. Children

Gleamly does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue at github.com/pvhung1403/gleamly/issues.
